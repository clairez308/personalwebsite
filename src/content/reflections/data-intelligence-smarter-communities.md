We just took 1st place ($3,000) at the Rogers x Databricks UBC Data Intelligence for Smarter Communities hackathon!

The challenge was to turn cell tower traffic data into smart community solutions focused on transit or security. We were given very limited data from three cell tower locations & (after much debate & discussions) decided on approaching it from a unique angle: focus on emergency disaster preparedness, where people would either want to go home or stay somewhere safe. We were so grateful for insights from Professor Bhushan Gopaluni. 

We focused on Waterfront Station, but we see the platform being upscaled in any location! We used Databricks to consolidate 8 million rows of data into a compact occupancy time series table, showing how many people are at Waterfront Station from different origin buckets. We also integrated open source datasets and APIs, including 25 community hubs in Vancouver, road closures from DriveBC, and TransLink GTFS Realtime data for transit status.

**Key learning:** Limited data can still lead to a big idea. Start from the real-world problem and let the data shape the solution.
