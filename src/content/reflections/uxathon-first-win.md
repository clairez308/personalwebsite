I'm still so happy about this one! 🎉 My team, Shreya Beas, Krupa Raval and I took 1st place at the UXathon this weekend, hosted by UBC UX Hub. We designed PREPAIRE, an AI-powered mock job interview platform with live feedback!

This one feels extra special because of how we got there. We started with an idea packed with features, but after a really valuable conversation with Naheel Jawaid, we realized it wasn't the right direction. So we scrapped the whole thing, 5 hours into the competition! It felt like a risky call, but it taught me to focus on what actually matters to the user.

A huge thank you to our mentors, Kylie Woo and Naheel Jawaid! You asked the tough questions and pushed us to refine our approach, and your insights really helped us build something we're proud of. This weekend was a reminder of how much teamwork, resilience and user-centered design can do together. I can't wait to bring these lessons into future projects!

**Key learning:** Be willing to scrap an idea when it isn't working. Focusing on what the user actually needs is worth more than adding features.
