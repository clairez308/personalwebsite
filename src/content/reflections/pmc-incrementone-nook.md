Our team just won the pitch competition at PMC x incrementOne at UBC! 🥳 We built Nook, a friends-only map for UBC students. You log places you've been with a quick verdict and a photo or short video, so your friends can check whether a spot they saw online is actually worth going to. Then you can turn a saved place into a group plan, pick a day, and lock it in once enough people say "I'm in."

What I'm most proud of is how we got there. We didn't start with the app. We started by talking to students! Seven of the eight we interviewed found the last new place they tried through another person, not a star rating. Of the six who named a barrier to trying somewhere new, four said it was having nobody to go with, or friends cancelling. Those two problems, trust and follow-through, became our focus.
