My first hackathon win! 🥳 SustainABite took 1st place in the beginner stream at youCode 2025, and I learned so much along the way!

I couldn't build a working backend in the beginner stream, and at first that felt like a setback. So I pivoted and focused on a solid MVP and frontend instead. Partway through, a teammate dropped out, and that was tough! But I didn't let it demoralize me, we kept going, and then we won!

Huge thanks to my teammates, Selina Fu and Gianna Li. You made the whole experience a lot of fun!

**Key learning:** Pivoting is not failing. A focused project you can actually finish beats an ambitious one you can't.
