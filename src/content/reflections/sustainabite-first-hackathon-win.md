My first hackathon win! 🥳 SustainABite took 1st place in SAP's beginner stream at youCode 2025, and I learned so much along the way!

We couldn't figure out how build a working backend in the beginner stream & at first that felt like a setback. So we pivoted and focused on a solid MVP and frontend instead. Partway through, a teammate dropped out, and that was defintely tough to push through, and we did - then we won!

Huge thanks to my teammates, Selina Fu & Gianna Li. You made the whole experience a lot of fun!

**Key learning:** Pivoting is not failing. A focused project you can actually finish beats an ambitious one you can't.
