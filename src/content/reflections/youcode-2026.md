I flew back from Toronto to Vancouver in April for youCode, and it was so good to be back for the weekend again! It was so cool to attend the event, and then move over to the organizer side so I could see it from the internal side!

Seeing it from the inside was a whole different experience from attending. There's so much that goes into a weekend like this, and I was really excited to be part of it.

**Key learning:** Seeing an event from both sides changes how you appreciate it. Each role teaches you something the other can't.
