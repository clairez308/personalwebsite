This was a huge full circle moment for me 🦄. This weekend I flew back from Toronto to Vancouver for youCode - this time as an organizer! It was so cool to have participated (+ won~) the event a year ago, and then move over to the organizer side (as logs + operations lead) so I could see it from the internal side.

Seeing it from the inside was a whole different experience from attending. There's so much that goes into a weekend like this, and I was really excited to be part of it.

**Key learning:** Seeing an event from both sides changes how you appreciate it. Each role teaches you something the other can't.
