We didn't know if Atlas would land. We'd spent the competition arguing ourselves out of features, betting that a system a judge could actually reason through would beat one that just looked more finished. When they called 3rd place, that bet felt validated in a way a grade or a rubric score never quite does.

The lesson stuck longer than the placement did. If I have to choose between polish and a decision I can defend, I'll take the defensible decision every time.
