Product Heist 2025 was my first pitching competition, and honestly, I didn't even know what a VC was going in! Venture capital was brand new to me, so I learned a lot from the judges and from my team.

We didn't know if Atlas would land! We spent the competition arguing ourselves out of features, betting that a system a judge could actually reason through would beat one that just looked more finished. When they called 3rd place, that bet felt validated in a way a grade or a rubric score never quite does.

The lesson stuck with me even more than the placement. If I have to choose between polish and a decision I can defend, I'll take the defensible decision every time!

**Key learning:** You don't need to know everything going in. Being able to defend your decisions matters more than polish.
