You don't need to understand 260+ muscles. You just need to understand your body!

I took the 2 hour train down to Mississauga from Toronto for BearHacks this weekend without knowing my team beforehand, and it was genuinely such a fun hackathon. Our team worked so well together!

At BearHacks, we built SOMA and won Best Use of Gemma 🏆

The idea was simple: when something feels off after a workout, most people don't want a textbook. They want a clear answer! In 36 hours, we got:

- a working 3D system
- conversational AI
- voice interaction

Shoutout to Noah Robertson, Farid Jamshid, and Paarth Sharma! Really strong team across product and engineering.

**Key learning:** If something is hard to understand, it's usually a design problem. Plain language and a clear answer go a long way.
