You don't need to understand 260+ muscles. You just need to understand your body!

I went to Mississauga for BearHacks without knowing my team beforehand, and it was genuinely such a fun hackathon. Our team worked so well together!

At BearHacks, we built SOMA and won Best Use of Gemma 🏆

The idea was simple: when something feels off after a workout, most people don't want a textbook. They want a clear answer!

So we redesigned the experience from the ground up:

- Grouped 260+ muscles into 20 functional muscle groups
- Built a 3D body that responds to your questions
- Plain language in, plain language out
- No clutter, no over-explaining

One thing we cared about a lot was privacy. We didn't want user queries being sent around unnecessarily, so we used Gemma to keep things lightweight and more controlled. That reduces how much data actually needs to be transmitted or stored.

In 36 hours, we got:

- a working 3D system
- conversational AI
- voice interaction

Stack: React, Three.js, Node.js, JavaScript, Gemma, ElevenLabs, Blender, Vercel, Render

Shoutout to Noah Robertson, Farid Jamshid, and Paarth Sharma! Really strong team across product and engineering.

**Key learning:** If something is hard to understand, it's usually a design problem. Plain language and a clear answer go a long way.
