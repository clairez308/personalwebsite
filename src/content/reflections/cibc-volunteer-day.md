Halfway through my internship, our team spent a day volunteering together instead of sitting at our desks. It's a small thing, but it was the first time I felt like part of the team rather than "the intern." The same people I'd spent the week reviewing vendor contracts with were the ones I was standing next to here.

The parts of an internship that actually change how you think about a place rarely show up on a resume line. This was one of them.
