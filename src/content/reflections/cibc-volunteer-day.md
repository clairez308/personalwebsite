Halfway through my internship, our team spent a day volunteering together instead of sitting at our desks! It's a small thing, but it was the first time I felt like part of the team instead of "the intern." The same people I'd spent the week reviewing vendor contracts with were the ones I was standing next to here!

The parts of an internship that actually change how you think about a place rarely show up on a resume. This was one of them.

**Key learning:** Connection with your team matters as much as the work itself. Showing up outside of work helps you feel like you belong.
