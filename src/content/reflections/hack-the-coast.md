Hack the Coast was monumental for me! I flew red-eyes from Toronto for three days in Vancouver and slept about 10 hours the whole weekend. I'd been working all year to plan SUS's first-ever hackathon, so it was worth every minute!

My co-lead Carmen Choo and I noticed something missing in the SUS community: the CS community was underserved. This event is how we fixed that. Carmen, thank you for taking a chance on me and letting me lead this, and for being such a reliable co-lead! We had late-night crash-outs and 1-1s together to make this happen, in the busiest exam season of the year.

Thank you to my subteam leads, Corbin, Gianna and Arnav, and to my entire team! It was so rewarding to hear people were shocked it was a first-time hackathon, because it ran so smoothly. 180 hackers came, and MLH was an amazing help. Huge thanks to Stephen from MLH for helping make our event a success and for relieving so much of the stress and burden!

**Key learning:** Big events come together because of the people around you. I'm so grateful for Carmen and my team for carrying this one with me.
