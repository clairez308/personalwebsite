We built Hack the Coast from nothing. Science students make up more than a quarter of UBC's undergrads and had never had a hackathon built for them, so as AVP Communications I helped put together SUS's first one, from scratch.

183 hackers showed up. Watching the room fill in during those first twenty minutes, after months of logistics and second-guessing whether anyone would actually come, is a specific kind of relief I don't think I'd felt before. The work that matters most is usually invisible until the moment it isn't.
