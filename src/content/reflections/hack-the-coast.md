The accumlation of 8 months of grinding, late-night crash outs, sometimes pouring up to 25 hours a week into the work... Hack the Coast was a huge success! Made it worth every minute. It was a sleepless weekend - flying red-eyes from Toronto for three days in Vancouver and sleeping about 10 hours the whole weekend. 

Our journey all started back in August - Carmen and I noticed something missing in the SUS community: the CS community was underserved (there was not a single event hosted by SUS, despite the undergrad body consisting of almost 50% of 10,000 students CS-adjacent studies). This event is how we fixed that. Carmen, thank you for taking a chance on me and letting me lead this, and for being such a reliable co-lead! 

Thank you to my subteam leads, Gianna, Arnav, Corbin, and to my entire team! It was so rewarding to hear people were shocked it was a first-time hackathon, because it ran so smoothly. 180 hackers came, and MLH was an amazing help. Huge thanks to Stephen from MLH for helping make our event a success and for relieving so much of the stress and burden!

**Key learning:** Big events come together because of the people around you. I'm so grateful for Carmen and my team for carrying this one with me.
